Floors are levels within Limbo that players progress the story through, serving as the 'chapters' for the game. These floors may contain different items and entities from the previous floor , along with brand-new locations . There are currently 2 released floors and 5 released subfloors , with at least 3 floors [ 1 ] [ 2 ] and 0 subfloors upcoming. [ 3 ] There are also 10 Visions, which are: Hotel- , Super Hard Mode , Retro Mode , Trick Or Treat , Cringle's Workshop , Daily Runs , Chaos Mode , Battle Mode , Rush Mode , And The Rooms .

Currently, the longest DOORS run consists of 1,670 rooms (1,678 rooms if counting starting rooms, The Archival Storage and Cobbler's Shop). Before beginning the run, the player must prepare a Lotus from The Outdoors and place it just before The Greenhouse . The player can then progress from The Backdoor through The Hotel and The Archives without skipping any sectors. While in The Archives, the player should look into a mirror to encounter Portrait and use it to duplicate the Skeleton Key that was used to access The Archives, as doing so fully recharges the key. The player can then continue through The Hotel again, The Outdoors, and The Mines, intentionally dying in The Mines in order to rebirth at The Greenhouse. From there, the player can finish The Hotel and access The Mines again, enter The Stairwell , and finally complete The Mines.
This run could however be indefinite by looping The Stairwell forever.
Guiding Light is the death messenger to the player in main floors , while Curious Light is the death messenger to the player in subfloors .
Though, Curious Light is shown to exist in all main floors .
Glitch is the death messenger in most visions.
DOORS has other event floors that are featured in other games: The Library?, The Library , Dark Library , The Outdoors?, and The Outdoors all from Tower Heroes; The Courtyard from Untitled Tag Game; Haunted Mansion , and Nightmare Mansion from Tales of Tanorio.
Subfloors have an inconsistent way of entering them. The Rooms and The Outdoors can be accessed from The Hotel, but The Backdoor can only be accessed if the player has already been to any of the other subfloors before and has the Detour badge, making it the only subfloor accessible directly from the Lobby without having to pay Stardust , as well as the only subfloor not accessible in a first run for new players.
In every main floor , the amount of open rooms not consumed by Void decreases at specific points of the floor.
This number is 5 from the start of the floor (Door X00), it decreases to 4 at Door X64, and finally, it decreases to 3 at Door X80, staying like that until the end. These points are the same for every main floor .
The third floor has been heavily rumored to be named "The Castle" or "The Dungeon."
The background for locked floors on the Floor Selection Screen is The Mines' background but darker.
A placeholder was once used for The Outdoors' background on the Floor Selection Screen. This was an image of the Jungle biome in Terraria.
DOORS has approximately 3,510 doors if combining all floors, subfloors and visions.
