" WHERE FORGOTTEN MEMORIES GO " - The quote in the trailer of The Archives
The Archives is an office-themed subfloor [ 1 ] that acts as a faithful reimagining and replacement to The Rooms . Its environments feature a more refined, atmospheric design with predominantly red tones, alongside spaces, storage areas, aquatic water, tables, and various scattered documents and objects.
Overview
In The Archives , you can find a lot of lore dispersed through books around the floor. The sections are different. They are marked by distinct lettered door numbers such as "A-09" or "C-38", giving the area an appearance of flee the facility dedicated to helping to forgotten information and memories. Each sector seems to have different sector variations such as Botanical Wing, Structural Anomaly, and other variations. Here are many brand new and exclusive entities that await for the players. The subdued lighting and unusual surroundings create an eerie, secluded ambience, fitting its role as the place where forgotten memories are kept. It is inspired by the music "911 / Mr. Lonely" by Tyler, The creator, as jasper_creations and also, stated on discord [ 2 ] .
This sub-floor also introduces new features such as water dispensers, computer keyboards, escalators, vending machines and a waiting system. There are also plenty of cupboards around that serve as hiding places, with some being damaged and presumably unusable, similar to The Rooms . However, all of the cabinets may contain shelves and items, such as file holders that are required to be removed before players can actually hide in them. A trolley holding a CRT television and a disc player below it, alongside a couple of discs can also be mainly seen in the trailer which had shown the teasers as footage. Given how The Archives was first officially teased through the television, it is possible that they can be interacted with & serve an important role through the floor, possibly with the discs holding footage of the "forgotten memories."
The floor also appears to have different sections through the rooms. The player can use certain Elevators which appear after every 50 rooms in each sector (for example:- Sector A, Sector B, Sector C, etc.). These sectors are divided by the waiting rooms shown, in which the player has to collect a ticket from the Teller and proceed to deal with the entity. They can also use gold to skip sectors, increasing in cost with every sector skipped.
Inside of the new stairwell expansion update, The Archives gained a pre-run shop, containing Pizza for 50 knobs, being able to get upgraded with stardust, Coin Roll (x4) for 150 knobs, giving 50 gold each, Flashlight for 100 knobs, and Vitamins for 100 knobs. Pre-run shop in The Archives only appears from using the skip in the lobby.
Entry
In Quick Play, go into an elevator in Lobby Menu of the Quick Play of the Lobby or click The Archives within the HOST GAME Lobby Menu section of the Lobby Menu . [ citation needed ]
Another way to enter Archives is finding a hidden door locked behind a Skull Lock and 2 locks which need lockpicking. [ citation needed ] (In total, 4 uses of the Skeleton Key and 2 Lockpicks to access the Archives) in room 60 of The Hotel (Previously, door lead to The Rooms , but with The Archives Update its destination was changed to The Archives)
Interior
In A-000, the player will find themselves inside a medium-sized room that looks like an office lobby. It has an exit to the exterior, as well as windows, chairs, two elevators, and an interactable computer. It also has a Shakelight Vending Machine.
The Archives is a huge office building with 26 different sectors, each having 50 doors and being represented by a letter ordered alphabetically. At the end of every sector, there is an elevator to continue progression through The Archives , that is controlled by a computer, which the player can pay Gold to skip sectors.
Starting in C-050, Emergency Exits (which will take the player to the room before The Infirmary) will begin to spawn at the end of every sector. There is also a unique exit at the end of the last sector, sector Z. Exiting or finishing The Archives will award the player two different Achievements.
Sectors Variations
Once entering any sector, players may read the terminal presented in front of the elevator upon first arriving. The sectors may have one of the following variations, in which some may affect the players' progression. There is also chat logs from the Drones, describing each of the variations of the sectors. However they do not seem to actually serve as tips for the players, as it is mostly about the Drones ' interaction and communication with each other regarding said variations.
The first sector can also have one of the variations, which can be discovered through the terminal in front of the first door. Most sectors may not have these variations, and are appointed with "No news at this time." However, the sectors may still have variations despite stating so.
Botanical Wing - a Sector Variant covered in Vines, Plants and vegetation and it's Overgrown. (look for "Botanical wing" in sector description).
Pizza Party - a Sector Variant with some rooms with party, decorations like Balloons, Colored Lights, Banners, increased Drones spawn rate and lots of Pizza on plates (look for " Come grab a slice of pizza ", " Pizza party! ", or " Good work everybody! " sector description).
Structural Anomaly - A Sector Variant with giant, half-empty rooms with anomalous geometry (look for any grammatical or punctuation errors in the description of the sector or "?").
This seems to be reminiscent of the Backdoor.
Ventilation Error - A Sector Variant that has slightly darker rooms and foggy areas (look for "Ventilation error" in sector description).
Windows/Surface Sector - A Sector Variant that has a significant amount of windows in its rooms.

Honcho is an important entity that appears in The Archives .
Appearance
Honcho appears as a flat humanoid entity seemingly made of paper wearing a light blue casual outfit with a white collar and sleeves seemingly rolled up, a blue tie, dark blue pants held up by brown straps, and brown shoes. It also has visible staples on its arms, likely resembling cartoonish arm hair. The limbs and torso can also bend and lightly fold in defining angles and are able to express flexible movements. Its legs are notably either folded horizontally inward, or have a piece attached behind it by the middle, likely for the entity to keep balance. It also has black 'tattoos' in his arms, resembling many Starlight symbols.
Its most prominent feature is its face and/or head, being a distinctive black square with choppy edges, giving it a foggy-like appearance, mainly resembling the redaction bars seen in documents. They are likely to be always facing the screen of the player and may appear animated. This could be covering its actual face and/or head, or that it is its head and is actually otherwise a physical manifestation of redaction, suggesting Honcho's role akin to reviewing and redacting documents.
In its old design, which was discovered through an accidental leak in October 2025, it only bared small changes with its outfit, having brown pants without straps but instead tied with a black belt. It also had visible pockets on the left side of its shirt and both side of its pants. Its head is a black square formed by smaller ones which fade around it. Its outfit was likely adjusted to fit more with the cooler tone of The Archives .
Behavior
Occasionally, when the player reaches the end of a sector, the elevator to the next sector will be overridden. This can happen as early as A-50, and as late as Y-50. Taking the elevator will lead the player to Honcho's executive office where he resides. Walking up to the desk will play a cutscene, displaying a graphic whereby Honcho orders the player to sort boxes into their respective depots. The graphic then disappears, and he throws the player down a pit in the floor. He will then jump down the pit and chase the player as they sort boxes into their corresponding areas. The player can override the doors to shut on Honcho as he chases them by holding an input key, giving them enough time to play the minigame. If the player is successful at finishing the boss fight before Honcho reaches them, a cutscene will play where Honcho grabs the player, stamps them, and smashes them through the floor, letting the player continue their run.
Notes
If any player has a Starlight Bottle in their inventory, Honcho will snatch it from them during the first cutscene, giving him a glowing yellow aura surrounding him after landing in the sorting room. Upon completing the entire sorting challenge, all players will be rewarded with the Star Employee achievement.
This content has spoilers for the ending of The Archives . If the players reach room Z-50 after the challenge is over, Honcho can be seen praying to Starlight .

Lore
According to its logs, Honcho is 213 years old. It hates The Archives and is desperate to leave but can't. It also seemingly has an obsession with Starlight , most likely believing it can take him out of the subfloor due to the Architect being present in every single floor, judging by the Starlight symbols on his arm, him taking the player's Starlight bottle and him praying to Starlight at Z-50 in star employee.

Ransom is an entity that has a chance of appearing in The Archives from Sector A and above. It is a rework of A-90 .
Appearance
Ransom's main initial appearance is a pixelated, monochromatic face that is heavily distorted. Its eyes are fully black, and it appears to be looking slightly upwards. However it can contort its face to a gaping, horrifying face straightforward at the player when attacking.
Its other notable appearance is a red, octagonal stop sign with a similar style to it, being pixelated and static.
It features a bright red theme behind it, as seen in computer screens, screen pop-ups and its jumpscare through the trailers.
Behavior
If encountered, Ransom will proceed to show up on the attacked player's screen using its passive state, flashing the screen with red static and playing a glitch noise. To survive, the player must make zero walking, turning, crouching or item-changing inputs onto the game. If the player is successful, Ransom disappears from screen. If the player fails to do so, Ransom screams at the player, followed by a download bar that quickly downloads Ransom's "malware" which hijacks the affected player's UI.
Several pop-ups fill the player's screen, along with the most notable pop-up displaying an image of Ransom crying. The text beside it reads "YOUR ITEMS HAVE BEEN ENCRYPTED" and "IF YOU DO NOT PAY THIS RANSOM BEFORE THE TIMER ENDS, YOUR ITEMS WILL BE UNRECOVARABLE BY ANY MEANS". The player's hotbar items are overlayed with the same red octagonal stop signs, and they cannot pick up or use any item while being ransomed. While any player is ransomed, all computer and television displays show a red static of Ransom . The affected player is given a timer of 01:30 minutes to collect 500 gold, as shown on the most notable pop-up. Chances of finding gold, and the rare Honey Pot scattered on the floor is increased at this time. However, the affected player cannot store any of this as the gold instead goes to paying for their ransom amount. The pop up will show the amout of gold needed left to pay for the ransom. If the player is successful in finding 500 gold, all pop-ups disappear and a single large pop-up displays the words "THANK YOU" with a green, octagonal thumbs-up logo. However, if the player fails at finding 500 gold before the timer runs out, Ransom will scream at the player, deal 75 damage and confiscate all the items from them.
Notes
When any player is being attacked by Ransom , all screens display its face, and the Teller 's screen will show a red screen with lines of code.
Therefore when players encounter Ransom and the Teller concurrently, it is advised to count the number of doors passed to hand the ticket to it at the correct door, as there will be no visible indication of the room called while Ransom hijacks all displays.

Trivia
Ransom's name and behavior are a reference to a type of malware called ransomware.
If Ransom's countdown timer appears while the player has a filled inventory, they will be unable to drop any items to pick up the Honey Pot and use it, consequentially forcing them to manually search for the remaining coins.
During the countdown, all containers have a significantly increased chance to contain coins, making the ransom easier to pay.
The Honey Pot that spawns because of Ransom's attack can only be found while the countdown is ongoing. Once the countdown ends, it despawns.
However, if the player picks up the Honey Pot before collecting the last coins needed for the ransom, the Honey Pot will stay in the player's inventory, allowing the player to smash it and collect an extra 500 coins.
Ransom can infect Noise , turning it into Hijack if you use the Disc .
When this happens, Ransom can start appearing in The Stairwell , but with a different behavior.
Ransom is the only Rooms entity rework to keep the same design as its predecessor, A-90 .
Ransom's theme takes several samples from Salinewin, a destructive Windows malware.

Drones are entities that appear in The Archives .
Appearance
Drones take forms of shadowy-like humanoid figures in completely black and chalky silhouettes, varying in either a masculine, feminine, or neutral form, and are mostly seen idling in a casual stance, animated with two frames or more. They emit a circular soft shadow beneath them. Multiple drones with the same appearances can appear in the same room.
Their faces are made up of colorful sticky notes that may have crudely drawn facial features, either eyes, a nose, and/or a mouth arranged to represent their face, with some of the sticky notes being blank.
Some of them may have less sticky notes only covering fewer features, while some may have a cluster amount with a complete set of features or at an excessive amount such as an additional eye. The drawings on the sticky notes also vary in different styles, with one of the eye on one of them being the same eye from another entity in The Archives , though it is unknown if they are connected or simply foreshadowing one another.
Behavior
Drones spawn in random positions in rooms and occassionally vanish and reappear in a new spot. When a player pushes a Drone too far, they will knock the player over dealing 5 damage and laughing in their face. If the player is holding an item and gets knocked by a Drone , that item gets dropped.
When the clocks located on walls strike 5:00 or 9:00, a distinctive chime can be heard from the speakers. This is very quickly followed by a horde of Drones that rush through the centre corridor of the room. If a player is caught by the hoarde, they get trampled, being dealt 30 damage and have a footstep mark on their face for a short while in the process. A stampede occurring will also alter the lighting, and can either make it a dim purple or foggy.
Spawn chances for Drones in a room increases when there is a "pizza party" or "come grab a slice of pizza" event in a sector.
Notes
Always take caution when going to the next room, as there could be Drones standing right behind the door.
Some of the doors in the Archives are see-through, so you can check if any Drones are standing directly behind the next door.
Drones will always spawn in rooms decorated for pizza parties.
Stampedes always take the shortest path to the next room.
Players can easily avoid getting trampled in the rush by hugging themselves against the side walls of the room.
While in some rooms there may be a path marked by the floor, this is misleading, as the stampede doesn't take that path.
A stampede may hit you through furniture, even if it's a safe spot for entities like Bash or Scribbles.
Singular Drones may appear in a bathroom stall.
This drone is usually the largest variant overall.
Walking very slowly into drones pushes them slightly away, making them also disappear a bit quicker.

Forget-Me-Nots appears in The Archives as a hostile entity.
Appearance
They appear as clusters of blue vines that entirely cover and block the doorway, featuring light blue flowers with yellowish eyeballs with a larger, main one in the center of the door. A blue vegetation similar to grass or thorns is noticeably on the floor in front of them.
Its flowers can also emerge from walls or ceiling around the door it is covering, which can grow and shrink.
Behavior
Forget-Me-Not can appear in any sector from Sector B onwards. It cannot appear in Sector A.
Forget-Me-Not is an entity that has a mini-game similar to that of Exit-8, where the player must memorize a completely normal room, then traverse through multiple rooms while checking if anything in them has changed or not.
When Forget-Me-Not spawns, the room before its actual room will always be a small room with two escalators, the left one moving down to the next numbered door, and the right moving upwards.
When the player enters Forget-Me-Not's actual room, it will resemble a metro station. Forget-Me-Not will be blocking the way through the next door. To pass through, you will need to look at 6 of Forget-Me-Not's little flowers that are scattered through the room. Once done, the big flower on the door that Forget-Me-Not is blocking will disappear.
Before entering the next room, all players should take a good minute or two to memorize the room. While every player is in this room, the clock will not tick down, even if no one is looking at one.
When a player interacts with Forget-Me-Not, they will push through them as if they were going through very thick tall grass, and end up inside Forget-Me-Not's mini-game. Each player has their own space inside Forget-Me-Not, and each player's space will almost never be the same as another players space, so never listen to your friends when they say what is different on their side, because it will seldom be true on yours (the anomalies are client-sided).
Each player must look around the room and try to spot any differences from the room they are in. If something is different from the real room, the player should backtrack. If nothing has changed, the player should move forth. If the player tries to go back when nothing changed, or go forth when something has changed, Forget-Me-Not will use its flowers to bite the player, taking away a good chunk of health. The total number of rooms you need to go through is not known, but assumed to be between 3-4.
When inside Forget-Me-Not, the clock continues to tick down, but the horde of drones does not pass through the metro station and does not hurt the player.

The Tellers are entities in The Archives .
Appearance
These entities appear to be a humanoid with its head being a TV monitor. On the top of its head are electrical plugs connected by wires hanging down, in a way that resembles hair. They wear a dark trenchcoat, and their right hands being a ticket puncher It appears to have a base with casters in place of its legs, possibly similar to an office chair.
They appear completely stationary and idle in front of a door. Its head displays a number with the text "Now serving" above it.
Behavior
The Tellers will always spawn in a unique room with a long, snaking but empty queue line. The Tellers will be standing at the next door in this room, blocking the way through. When the player first enters this room, the Teller powers up.
When a player walks up to The Tellers, it will print a ticket in their mouth, take it out, and hold it out to the player. The player must take this ticket. If multiple people are present, Teller will print out each players respective ticket one at a time. Each ticket printed will always be a higher number then the last.
Once every player has a ticket, The Tellers will move out of the way and stand next to the door. Every time a new numbered door is opened, The Tellers will move through it and go stand next to the next numbered door, then increase a number on it's screen. Every roof or wall monitor will also show the number that The Tellers has.
Once The Tellers reach a number that any player has on their ticket, it will block the following door. The Teller' s screen will then start to fill with a red color over the course of 7 seconds. The player with the same number ticket as Tellers' number must hand over their ticket to the Tellers . If the ticket is not handed over by the time their screens are fully red, The Tellers will chase the player.
The player can hide in a closet to avoid The Tellers if The Tellers are in chasing mode. If the player is in a closet, The Tellers will walk up to the closet, take the player's ticket, and power off without dealing any damage to the player.
The player can dodge the The Tellers in chasing mode eventually allowing the door to open. However, the tellers eventually will catch up no matter what and it will be faster.
Multiple Tellers can spawn in a sector but not when there's an active teller.
Interactions with other entities
When the Tellers comes into contact with a ForgetMeNot door, the flowers will cover it.
When Ransom is active, The Tellers' screens will become red and corrupted, showing Ransom's pop-ups.
If Bash or Scribbles spawn while the Tellers is active, they are able to open the next door, and increase the The Tellers' number. This could be dangerous for the player because Teller's countdown may start while the player is still hiding in the last room.
It is possible for more than one Teller to be active at once, causing the player to have to keep track of multiple tickets. This will also change the color of the second Teller to yellow, third Teller to green, and the fourth to aqua/blue. There is a bug where if a teller ends the door before another teller, the next teller will transfer to the next color while never having two tellers present at once.

Bash is an entity in The Archives and is a rework of A-60 .
Appearance
Bash' s render is similar to A-60 .
When comparing the two representations of Bash, we see a distinct evolution between the in-game model (in game) and the stylized journal sketch (in journal).
In the in-game screenshot (in game), the entity is presented in an overwhelming crimson hue, matching the description of a "more red" face. The key features are intense and dramatic: eyes that burn with blinding, fiery energy, dynamic and complex fire effects, and a wide maw filled with glowing teeth. The facial structure is defined, but the color saturation is extremely high, dominating the scene.
In the journal sketch (in journal), the representation is more schematic. The entity is drawn with a pale tan or dark orange appearance, displaying a deeply angry expression with distinctly drawn teeth and eyes blazing with stylized fire. The text accompanying this image explicitly confirms it is an "Orange entity" with "fire bursting from its eyes." This version is a more subdued, graphic interpretation of the 3D model seen in-game.
Behavior
Bash's behavior is similar to Rush 's behavior, but has several key differences.
Before the entity's appearance, a distant roaring noise can be heard that grows louder as the entity nears the room the player is in. Upon its arrival from previous rooms, Bash rushes through the main corridor of the rooms, despawning at the end of the following, unoccupied room. The entity is also significantly fast, going almost double the speed of Rush.
While the entity is present, players will need to find a safe space to hide in. This includes lockers , within the walls of enclosed desks, or in separate side rooms. However, if they fail to do so, Bash sets them of fire, dealing 35 damage in the process. If a player is standing on either carpet or grass (botanical sections), Bash can still do fire damage even if they are not in his direct line of sight.
Using exploits, you can see that Bash is crucifiable, however it has no animation for getting crucified.

Alma is an entity that appears in "The Archives".
Appearance
Alma presents as a dark humanoid figure that appears to resemble a young adult or adolescent. She is entirely in grayscale and surrounded by black shadows. She has pale skin, black hair, and is covering her eyes with her hands.
When attacking the player, Alma uncovers her eyes and seems to be crying. Her arms stretch out towards you and her mouth opens, emitting a high pitched screaming noise.
Behavior
Alma spawns beginning at Sector J in The Archives.
Alma is guaranteed to spawn at some point in each sector after the player's first encounter with her. Alma will always spawn in a special room, usually early into the sector. Several rooms before her actual room, crying/weeping can be heard up ahead. It starts out quiet, and the closer the player is to Alma's room, the louder it will get. Upon reaching Alma's room, the crying sound will change to a different crying sound.
Alma's room is very dark. Players walk into the room onto a small upper level that wraps around half the room. Alma can be seen sitting in the center of the room, surrounded by what seem to be dead Drones, under a single light. When any player walks down the stairs and onto the floor that Alma is on, Alma will look at the player, before disappearing.
Alma will almost always spawn at least once per sector. She can spawn at anytime during the sector as well. When Alma spawns, she will be out of view from the player. Crying sounds can be heard from Alma while she is active. Alma will circle around the player in an attempt to be seen by the player. A good way to avoid looking at her is by looking down at the floor and turning away from Alma when she tries to move into focus. If the player looks at Alma for more than a second, she will become enraged. If the player can avoid looking at Alma for long enough, she will eventually fade away.
When Alma becomes enraged, it will become aggressive and attempt to dash at the player, doing this about 3-5 times before disappearing. If Alma catches the player with a dash or they enter a closet, a minigame similar to Figure's closet minigame will occur where you have to tap to keep an eye icon within a growing/shrinking eye icon, after a bit it will disappear. If the eye icon gets out of the boundary in the minigame, Alma will deal continuous damage that can be potentially fatal.
Trivia
If Alma spawns before the Honcho sequence, she may appear during it, causing massive issues.
The word "Alma" means "apple" in Hungarian, Turkish and Kazakh, and "soul" in Spanish and Portuguese.

Portrait is an entity in The Archives which only appears under certain circumstances.
Appearance
Under its disguise , it looks exactly the same as the player's avatar.
Portrait's true appearance is a gray Roblox avatar with concrete-like texture. Its eyes are two simple black dots and has a nose protruding out of its face.
Behavior
Portrait permeates in the mirrors throughout The Archives' s bathrooms. In this form, it looks identical to the player and will mimic their behavior, including: moving around, holding items, looking around, etc., giving the illusion of a normal mirror. However, when the player stares at the mirror for around 20 seconds, the mirror will begin to crack, revealing its true appearance. After the animation is done, Portrait will completely disappear from that mirror and the crack will disappear.
From this point onward, Portrait will have a chance to appear outside of the mirrors under its true look and copy the players' actions. When it holds an item, that item will also have the Marble Material applied on it. In this state, it is completely harmless, even if the player decides to walk into it or try to interact with it in any ways. It has a chance to disappear if it escapes the player's view.
While Portrait is mimicking the player holding an item, holding the interact button on Portrait's item will cause it to manifest into reality, duplicating the player's item. This can be done twice before Portrait shatters into pieces and is removed from the room. Portrait will stop appearing after this.
To unlock the "Annihilation" badge, the player must activate Portrait in a mirror at The Archives and then get back to The Hotel. Only when back at The Hotel, try to use the Portrait to duplicate the Crucifix.

Room Mechanics
Elevators take certain amounts of gold ( 100, 125, etc. ) to skip a sector and proceed to the next (example: going from A to F), costing 100 gold in the first upgrade and increasing the next ones by 25% of the previous amount (e.g. 125 to 156 instead of 125 to 150), but only in that sector. Closets function as a chest, containing books that are tossed out once interacted with, and also sometimes rewarding Gold, Stardust, and/or items such as Paper Airplanes, Bandages, Pizzas and Lunchboxes. When the books are thrown out, the closet's doors become slightly ajar and it functions as a regular hiding spot.

The Archives has a bit of important lore setup for Floor 3 and the main overarching story. [ 6 ]
The main reason for the replacement of The Rooms with The Archives is to increase the floor's replayability, as the developers and the community thinks that The Rooms had a very dull and unexciting gameplay. [ 7 ]
Another reason is to disprove theories about DOORS and nicorocks5555 's Rooms being connected, and to fully add The Archives into the game's canon. [ 8 ]
The floor was originally planned to release in November 2025, however as the team decided to change the way they release updates, the major update was moved ahead to early 2026 before being delayed again to August 28, 2026.
The teasers which were shown as old VHS footages, with realistic first person view movements and the eeriness of the rooms were inspired by Kane Pixels' Backrooms series. [ 9 ]
Interestingly enough, sectors with classification of "Structural Anomaly" shows that most of its rooms have structures similar to that of Kane Pixel's depiction of the Backrooms.
One of the rooms shown in the game was inspired by SCP Containment Breach, which was suggested by jasper_creations to RediblesQW . [ 10 ]

S-50 has this side room called "Archival Storage" which is a storage room containing rows of document boxes. An adjacent Vacuum opening, originally connected to The Stairwell , leads to the 'Who Am I?' badge cutscene. Said cutscene will portray the Player blacking out and waking up to reveal that they went insane, scribbling on dozens of sheets of paper scattered across the room. Archival Storage also has a side room that contains an old laptop with the original Ransom virus on it, of which can be ejected as a CD to obtain as an item. This CD can then later be used in The Stairwell on one of Noise 's TVs to infect it with Ransom, spawning the entity Hijack, which will get the player the REDACTED badge. Note: Doing this will also allow Ransom to spawn in The Stairwell if the player then survives and escapes the landing where they spawned Hijack.
There is a sign that points to the Archival Storage in room A-000.
Z-50
This content has spoilers for the ending of the Archives ..
Z-50, is the true ending and Last door of The Archives . It is room 1300 of the Archives. It is one of the biggest rooms in this floor due to the large void-like area, where it contains a large part of it broken off and leading to an exit door, with a brick stair (similar to the one found in the Lobby , highly speculated to be the entrance for Floor 3 due to similar textures (although this isn't confirmed) leading to a blue portal, the end of the archives, along with the Suitcase . Entering the portal will bring the player back to The Hotel as it is the end of the Archives.
If you did Star Employee earlier in the run, Honcho can be seen bowing down to Starlight once you reach this room.
A-000
A-000 is first entrance and the spawn room of the Archives. A hidden entryway room in the secret maze sub-room of The Hotel at Door 60 requires a Skeleton Key and 2 Lockpicks , which is how you access the Archives without doing it straight from the Hotel.
