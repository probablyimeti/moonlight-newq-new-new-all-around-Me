The Outdoors is a subfloor accessible via The Greenhouse in The Hotel or via the Lobby to players who have discovered the subfloor through The Hotel [ 1 ] . In The Outdoors , players traverse a large garden-themed expanse, with their progress being marked by meters instead of doors. The subfloor was released on August 10th, 2025, on the exact third anniversary of DOORS .
Overview
Entry
The Outdoors is a subfloor for The Hotel . The entrances to the subfloor are located in the first room of the Greenhouse (Door 90) and at the end of the Greenhouse (Door 99). Entering requires vines to be cut by either Shears or a Multitool , and a Skeleton Key to unlock the Skull Lock.
The Outdoors is not accessible with Modifiers active as a red barrier will block both entrances.
Exterior
The Outdoors takes place outside in a dark, forest-like environment during a thunderstorm (similar to The Hotel ). The Outdoors includes many different structures such as graveyards, cabins, and monuments.
This content has spoilers for the end of The Outdoors
. After leaving The Hedge Maze, the player walks towards an overlook of a river and gets attacked by Bramble . Before it can kill them, though, the Groundskeeper appears and dropkicks the entity, leading to all three of them falling into the river. Bramble reaches for the player again but gets stopped by the Groundskeeper, who continues to fight it, while the player falls down a sewer pipe leading to Mines Jeff Shop .
Entities
Entities are the player 's main hindrance in The Outdoors . Most of them are hostile or, in the Groundskeeper 's case, at least can be. However, a few of them are helpful or, in the case of the Caws , have the potential to be.

The Groundskeeper is a recurring entity in The Outdoors . He was teased in the 11th Devlog , then in the reveal teaser for The Outdoors . He was fully revealed in the trailer for The Great Outdoors Update , and released alongside it on August 10 th , 2025.
Appearance
The Groundskeeper appears as a humanoid wearing a black straw hat and scarf, brown overalls over a gray t-shirt, a gray belt with a keychain on the back of it, black gloves, and a pair of black boots. His head is dirty and gray. His arms are likely bones and covered in vines. The Groundskeeper has two glowing yellow eyes, with one of them squished and possibly hollow in the center. He carries a hollow metal scythe with a wooden handle.
Behavior
Upon opening a door , the Groundskeeper has a chance of spawning either walking around or in an outhouse. In the latter case, his scythe will be leaning against the outhouse. After an unknown period of time, the Groundskeeper leaves the outhouse and begins roaming the area.
The Groundskeeper usually takes care of the area around him. He cuts weeds if there are any, and plants bugleweeds. Otherwise, he roams the area for an unknown period of time before returning to his outhouse.
Whenever he notices a player on the grass, the Groundskeeper grunts and begins approaching them. If they stay on the grass, the Groundskeeper will shout "Hey" or yell, and start running towards them. Once he reaches the player on the grass, he damages them with his scythe before killing them. If they step off the grass beforehand, the Groundskeeper will stop and do a hostile gesture at the player before returning to his usual behavior.
Whenever he spots a player near him, the Groundskeeper turns to face them as he walks until they go away. He can also do a hostile gesture at the player. Whenever he notices a player's corpse, he walks towards the dead body and wraps it in a sack. Whenever he spots an active Mandrake , the Groundskeeper runs towards the plant in an attempt to kill it. The Groundskeeper can point at Monuments for players.
At the end of Eyestalk 's chase sequence, the Groundskeeper cuts the entity in half before it can attack the player. He carries one half of the entity and looks at the player before walking away.
Upon entering The Hedge Maze , the Groundskeeper spots the player on the grass and shouts at them. When he tries to approach them, Bramble ambushes him and drags him away. At the end of The Outdoors , the Groundskeeper dropkicks Bramble before it can kill the player, leading to him, Bramble, and the player falling into a nearby river. It proceeds to fight Bramble, but it is unknown who won.
Notes
The Groundskeeper can not be angered while he is cutting weeds, planting bugleweeds, doing a hostile gesture, or killing another player.
The Groundskeeper can not attack players in toolsheds .
The Groundskeeper can spot players who step on the grass whether they are above or below it.
After a player steps off the grass, the Groundskeeper can still attack them for a brief moment.
Jumpscares
1st and 3rd Person
The Groundskeeper slashes the player with his scythe, dealing 35 damage, and knocks them to the ground. He looks down at them before crushing their head under his foot.
Alternatively, the Groundskeeper impales the player with his scythe, dealing 60 damage, and discards them behind him.

Caws are entities/animals that appear in The Outdoors . They serve as a warning of approaching entities like Surge or the Groundskeeper.
Appearance
Their appearance is similar to Pied Crows with dark gray plumage/feathers and a white underbelly. The have eyes similar to Louie 's. Caws are friendly entities.
Behavior
Upon entering a room, Caws have a chance of spawning; in which case, they'll either be resting on the ground or perching on an object such as a lamppost or a tree. When a player gets close to one, it'll make a loud cry before flying up into the sky. Although at first Caws may seem just as an environmental entity with no impact on gameplay, they have two secret functionalities. For one, they will tend to perch near Lotus Petals and Iron Keys , [ 7 ] acting as an indicator for when one is present. Additionally, upon feeding one bread they will boost the spawn rate of Lotus Petals and Iron Keys in addition to providing the "Feed The Birds" achievement
Trivia
The Caws are named after the word "caw," which is "the loud, rough cry of a bird such as a crow".
Grampy refers to the Caws as "Tweeties".
The Caw is likely based on the hooded crow.
Their cry however appears to be similar to American crows.
Players can gain the "Feed the Birds" achievement by dropping or equipping bread near a Caw. [ 8 ]

The Mandrake is a recurring hostile entity in The Outdoors . It was initially revealed in Lightning Splash âs profile picture, teased in the outdoors teaser trailer, teased in the 37th devlog, and then teased in the trailer for The Great Outdoors Update .
Appearance
Mandrakes appear as pale beige turnip-shaped creatures. Their mouths are lined with six gray teeth at the top and four at the bottom. Mandrakes have white glowing eyes and three green leaves sprouting from the top of their heads. They're wrapped in pale green thorny vines. Whenever the Groundskeeper kills a Mandrake , its eyes and teeth disappear.
Behavior
A Mandrake is initially stationary and poking out of the ground. Once a player gets close to it or enters its line of sight, the Mandrake uproots itself, flies towards, and begins wailing at the player, which forces them to look at it. After about ten seconds in the Mandrake 's presence, the player suffers a âheart attack,â falls unconscious, and dies. [ 9 ] If another player is nearby, the Mandrake will detach itself from its vine, roll towards, and begin wailing at them. Players can pick up an active Mandrake , which detaches it from its vine, and replant it in the hole it uprooted itself from in order to subdue it. If the Groundskeeper âs nearby, he'll run towards the Mandrake and impale it with his scythe to kill it. Players can also run away from an active Mandrake , though it'll detach itself from its vine to follow them. If they get far enough, though, it'll roll back into its hole instead.
Notes
Mandrakes can spawn as early as the first door, though it's extremely rare. [ 10 ]
Mandrakes can spawn in ponds.
Despite Curious Light stating that they induce heart attacks, Mandrakes actually cause players to lose oxygen similarly to Firedamp and Floods .

Surge is a recurring hostile entity in The Outdoors . It was teased in the 37th Devlog and then in the trailer for The Great Outdoors Update .
Appearance
Surge appears as six yellow and white scribbles resembling a mouth and two eyes. These seem to represent electric shockwaves. In-game, these scribbles are constantly shifting.
Behavior
One or two lightning strikes briefly turning the sky lime sometimes occur, warning players of Surge 's upcoming attack. Once it does, a burst of lightning turning the sky yellow begins, and players who are uncovered gain a fuzzy yellow vignette around the edge of their screens. Surge spawns hundreds of studs in the sky.
Surge constantly moves towards players or erratically circles them if unable to reach them. Uncovered players who are near Surge take 40 damage per tick. After about 10 seconds, Surge loses the ability to damage players and ascends to the sky to despawn. The yellow vignette disappears at this point, and the constant lightning ends.
Notes
Surge 's attacks are not directly tied to opening doors . Instead, it spawns on a timer.
Surge can spawn multiple times in the same area because of this, even if every player is dead.
Surge spawns more frequently if a door is not opened for too long.
Surge can not spawn in the following conditions unless one of its lightning strikes has already occured:
The first door has not been opened.
The Groundskeeper spawned in the last opened room.
The last opened room is part of Eyestalk 's chase, including The Clearing and the ending room.
The Mill has been reached.
Instead of using raycasting, Surge damages players who are unsheltered and are near it at the same time.
Each player has 10 spots that are six studs away from them. The game checks these spots for walls and overhead cover. If none are clear, the player is sheltered. If at least one is clear, the player is exposed and thus vulnerable to Surge .
Strategy
To avoid taking damage from Surge , players should enter a toolshed or a roofed area such as a cabin, a large cave, or an unlocked crypt. Since Hide is not present in The Outdoors , players do not need to wait for Surge to get close before entering a toolshed.
Most cabins and toolsheds have grass around their entrances. If the Groundskeeper is present, players should mind its location while trying to hide from Surge .
Jumpscares
1st Person
Surge briefly appears in the center of the player 's screen, flashing it yellow.
3rd Person
Surge zaps the player and leaves a corpse with the texture of lava. The corpse twitches and emits sparks.
Trivia
Surge may be inspired by ball lightning. One theory suggests that ball lightning is composed of heavy positive and negative ions in the air formed during a regular lightning strike. Under the influence of electrical forces, they coalesce into a ball and can coexist for quite a long time until their water shell is destroyed. Surge emerges from a cluster of lightning bolts and hovers near the player, similar to the movement of ball lightning.
Surge may also be inspired by Sorrow from Grace, as they are both sky-related entities with similar mechanics.
Grampy refers to Surge as a "Zapper."
Grampy saying "Zappers" implies that there are multiple Surges in existence.

The Monument is a recurring hostile entity in The Outdoors . It was teased in the 7th Devlog , where one can be heard killing a player .
Appearance
Monuments initially appear as gray concrete obelisks. They are full of red flesh on the inside, and so are their bases.
Whenever active, Monuments extend their four red fleshy pointy legs from the bottom of their bodies. Each leg has a concrete plate on its outer-facing side.
Behavior
A Monument spawns and sits on top of its base. Once unobserved, it dismounts from its base, stands up, and begins walking towards the nearest player . The Monument moves faster the longer that no one's looking at it. Whenever observed again, it quickly retracts its legs and stands still until no one's looking at it. Then, it stands up again and resumes moving.
Upon reaching a player, the Monument stabs them before dragging them under itself and crushing them to death. This can not be stopped, even if other players are watching the Monument .
Monuments produce audible breathing that players can hear by standing next to one. While moving, Monuments produce audible footsteps.
Notes
Monuments can not attack players in toolsheds , but they can walk up to one.
Monuments despawn upon triggering Eyestalk âs chase sequence, talking to Grampy, or entering The Hedge Maze .
Looking at a Monument through a window does not count as observing it.
Jumpscares
1st and 3rd Person
The Monument knocks the player over and stabs them with its front-right leg. It drags them below itself before crushing them to death.
Trivia
At The Mill , a dead Monument can be seen roasting on a campfire.
Monuments may be inspired by weeping angels, who appear as statues and also stop moving while being observed.
Monuments are actually physically only the meaty inside, and its appearance as a real monument is because it uses hollowed out monuments as a shell. [ 12 ]
In a rare subroom, a Monument can be seen struggling against the World Lotus trying to control it.
Monuments may be based on a scrapped entity that was meant to appear in The Mines . This entity would have disguised itself as a small locker.
Monuments used to be the only entities in The Outdoors that are spawnable via the Admin Panel .
Grampy refers to the Monuments as "Pillarm[e]n."
Out of all the entities, Monument is the one that tastes the most like a french fry. [ 13 ]

Eyestalk is a major hostile entity in The Outdoors . It was teased in the 36th Devlog .
Appearance
Eyestalk appears as an aspen tree trunk with its lower half wrapped in pale green thorny vines. These vines form arms and legs, though its lower left-arm is an aspen branch. On the right side of its head, Eyestalk has two more branches with two small orange leaves growing off each one.it should also be noted that eyestalk has vines wrapped around the top of the aspen log body it has.Moreover it seems that eyestalk has eyes drawn onto him.
Behavior
Eyestalk 's appearance is foreshadowed by the appearance of aspen trees. Once players reach the end of The Clearing , Eyestalk forms itself and begins chasing them throughout the following areas. Upon reaching a player, Eyestalk restrains them with vines before smacking them twice, killing them. It screams afterwards. Eventually, Eyestalk catches up to the player after they stumble from a jump, but the Groundskeeper cuts it in half before it can smack them. One half of Eyestalk tries to grab the player's leg before the Groundskeeper picks it up and carries it away, while the other can be interacted with. For the crossings, players should glance both ways for a path forwards before heading straight. In this case, carrying a light source is recommended for spotting and avoiding instances of it. A lantern is guaranteed to spawn near the end of The Clearing .

Bramble is a major hostile entity in The Outdoors . It serves as the subfloor's final boss, roaming The Hedge Maze in a manner akin to Figure .
Appearance
Bramble appears as a tall humanoid made largely from pale green thorny vines, with pieces of a black lamppost inside its left leg, torso, and forearms. On its left shoulder, it has a small pink lotus flower. It has four fingers on its right hand and three toes on its right foot.
Bramble 's head is a lantern that can horizontally split open to reveal its tongue. The lantern can also turn on and off.
Behavior
Initially, Bramble ambushes the Groundskeeper and drags them into the bushes. Then, it roams The Hedge Maze at a slow pace as its lantern head occasionally turns on and off.
While the lantern is on, Bramble quickly moves towards players who it sees moving. Meanwhile, the player gets attacked by thorns that each deal small bits of damage until they stop moving, hide in a toolshed, or make Bramble lose sight of them. If the player continues to move, Bramble will kill them upon touching them.
While the lantern is off, Bramble can not attack players.
Whenever it is far away from any players, Bramble retreats into the ground and discards its current lamppost, leaving it broken. Afterwards, it possesses a new one that is closer to a player.
Notes
Bramble can possess broken lampposts again.
Bramble can not see players over hedges.
Bramble seems to actually be able to teleport to any light source, not just lanterns. This is shown in Chaos Mode in this footage : [1]

Non-exclusive Entities
World Lotus - The main antagonist of the subfloor.
Snare - Deals 20 non-lethal damage to a player and holds them in place for a few seconds.
Starlight - A death messenger to the player after dying in subfloors .
Glitch - Acts as a failsafe and attacks players that are lagging too far back from the rest of the team when in a multiplayer subfloor elevator.
Glitch Fragment Entities - Glitched variations of Screech , Rush and Ambush that appear when interacting with a Glitch Fragment .
Portrait - Appears if a player brings Portrait from The Archives into The Outdoors , or shatters a pocket mirror .
Non-exclusive Hazards
Floods - Common hazards that can drain the player's oxygen, causing them to drown.
Puzzles
The Hedge Maze
A map of The Hedge Maze.
The Hedge Maze appears in the last 250m of The Outdoors . Players must escape through the other side of it while avoiding Bramble . However, the exit is blocked by one of the World Lotus ' roots. To remove it, players must use three guillotines, or "traps" as Starlight calls them, to cut its other roots. Afterwards, players can leave The Hedge Maze and escape The Outdoors .

Room Mechanics
Guaranteed Rooms
The Small Courtyard : The starting room of the The Outdoors . The room has a fountain with the Caws sitting on it. Look down for more information.
The Clearing : The starting room of the Eyestalk Chase, a bunch of aspen trees are found in there, with some trees cut down. The log in the middle is Eyestalk before the chase is initiated.
The Mill : The room where Grampy resides. Giving him eight lotus petals will result in making a lotus . Look down for more information.
The Hedge Maze : The last room of The Outdoors . It is a huge maze where the player /s have to cut three huge vines with the three guillotines, whilst Bramble tries to pursue the player/s.
Large Gates
A player opening a gate.
There are gray metal gates throughout The Outdoors . These can be opened by pressing a nearby button. Afterwards, a short noise will play before the gate opens. Players may be able to take alternate routes.
Shears/Toolshed Rooms
These rooms contain small toolsheds, containers that will always hold the Shears .
The Small Courtyard : The starting room of The Outdoors , where you enter out of The Greenhouse . The Shears can either be found on the right or left side of the room against the wall or on the grass. This room also contains a vine-covered chest on the left side of the room resting beside a tree.
The Mill : This is the room with Grampy in it, found not too far from The Hedge Maze, the final room. The Shears are found toward the left of the room next to the hiding closet under the cover of the balcony of the first building on the left.
The Bridge : A room with a bridge connecting two sides of a ravine. The Shears are located on an island in a lake at the bottom of said ravine. This area is accessed via a grassy unmarked ramp section toward the left of the room. In this area there are also five vine-covered chests (1st is located in front of you when you reach the bottom of the hill, 2nd and 3rd are hidden in the 'vine-blocked loot area', 4th and 5th are located at the other side of the lake on the island), two lanterns (1st is slightly off the beaten path and 2nd is in the 'Vine-Blocked loot area'), and one crypt with a lotus petal (located on the left corner of the lake-shore). The bridge room is known as "Bridge_1" in the game files (so you don't get confused which bridge room will always contain the Shears).
The Water Tower Room : This is the room where there is a water tower at the low elevated area of the room and the tree falls over near the water tower. The Shears are on the higher elevated area of the room where (if you go up from the water tower section) are located slightly to the right in the corner of a plausible sub-room. This room also has 2-3 Vine-covered chests (one located next to the fallen tree and one to two located in the higher elevated section slightly off the beaten path).
The Graveyard : This room is identifiable because it has a crypt (the small room you unlock with the Iron Key ) and a "vine-blocked loot area" (The small area in some (sub-)rooms that has an entrance that has been obstructed by vines, inside is usually two to three vine chests along with one or more Lanterns ). The Shears are in the corner diagonally opposite from the 'Vine-Blocked loot area', they are also on the same side of the grass as the crypt.
Crypt Rooms
These rooms contains crypts which contains Lotus Petals that are unlocked by Iron Keys .
The Graveyard : This room is identifiable because it has a crypt (the small room you unlock with the Iron Key ) and a "vine-blocked loot area" (The small area in some (sub-)rooms that has an entrance that has been obstructed by vines, inside is usually two to three vine chests along with one or more Lanterns ).
The Large Graveyard : This room is similar to the The Graveyard, but it has two crypts instead of one. it Also has a Second Floor wich has 2 entrances, A Gate and a vine-infested path.
The Small Graveyard : This room contains a small stone room that is connected to a small graveyard that contains a crypt through a gate.
The Bridge : A room with a bridge connecting two sides of a ravine. This area is accessed via a grassy unmarked ramp section toward the left of the room. In this area there are also five vine-covered chests (1st is located in front of you when you reach the bottom of the hill, 2nd and 3rd are hidden in the 'vine-blocked loot area', 4th and 5th are located at the other side of the lake on the island), two lanterns (1st is slightly off the beaten path and 2nd is in the 'Vine-Blocked loot area'), and one crypt with a lotus petal (located on the left corner of the lake-shore).
Flooded Rooms
These rooms contain a flood which is deep enough to drown the player. In The Outdoors , there is only one type of flooded room, containing a large pond in the center of a garden-type room. However, the player is only able to drown if they crouch in the pond.
Special Locations
The Small Courtyard (0 Meters)
The Small Courtyard.
The Small Courtyard is the starting area of The Outdoors . The first part of this area is a brick structure containing flower beds, a wooden gate leading outside The Greenhouse , and paned windows reaching up to the structure's roof on each side of the gate. Outside the brick structure, there is the titular small courtyard containing a fountain, which is guaranteed to have Caws perching on it, a vine-covered chest, and a small toolshed with Shears inside. At the front of the courtyard, there is a metal gate. Opening it leads to a small forest with stone ruins and a dirt path leading to the next area. On the right side of a forest, a tree will always fall over.
The Clearing (1,200 Meters)
The Clearing.
The Clearing appears about halfway through The Outdoors . This area's entrance is a metal gate. Opening it leads to the titular clearing surrounded by fallen and upright aspen trees, with a tree trunk lying near a lamppost in the center. A Lantern will always spawn near the end of this area. Upon reaching the end of The Clearing, Eyestalk forms itself using the aforementioned tree trunk and begins chasing players through the following areas.
While evading Eyestalk, players can encounter a variety of obstacles, including: a three-way junction with only one correct path forwards, a cabin, a patio containing desks blocking the way and instances of Snare , a brick wall with an opening to crouch through, and a river with two bridges going across it.
Eventually, the player jumps from a broken bridge and falls into a ditch. Panicked, the player tries to scramble out, but falls deeper into the ditch, allowing Eyestalk to catch up. Just as Eyestalk jumps down, the Groundskeeper cuts it in half before it can attack the player. One half of Eyestalk tries to grab the player's leg, but the Groundskeeper picks it up and, after a short look at the player, walks away. This marks the end of Eyestalk's chase sequence.
The Mill (2,100 Meters)
The Mill.
The Mill appears in the last 300â400m of The Outdoors . [ 19 ] This area contains the titular mill with a pool of water behind it and a small wooden structure in front of it, where a Lantern and a toolshed containing Shears can be found.
At the front of the mill, there is an overhang that Grampy resides under. Inside the mill, there is a bed with another Lantern beside it, a bunch of wooden boxes, an unfinished guillotine, a conveyor belt with Eyestalk's other half on top of it, and a few of the signs seen throughout the rest of The Outdoors . On the mill's left half, there is a large passage and a door leading outside the building. To the left of the mill, there are a few aspen trees lying on the ground.
Near the end of this area, there is a campfire with a Monument 's corpse beside it and one of its legs being roasted on top of it. Players can spin the leg around by interacting with it.
The Hedge Maze (2,250 Meters)
The entrance to The Hedge Maze.
The Hedge Maze appears in the last 250m of The Outdoors . This area's entrance is a metal gate leading to an arbor covered in vines and bugleweeds. Beyond this, there is a patio containing a statue of a horse or a large Caw.
The maze itself is surrounded by trees and full of the World Lotus ' roots. The entity itself resides in the center of The Hedge Maze, spreading its roots outwards above the maze. Throughout The Hedge Maze, there are more metal gates, tall obelisks, and toolsheds for players to hide in.
Past the exit to The Hedge Maze, there is an overlook of a river leading into a sewer pipe.

Bugs
The signs that say the remaining distance until the exit may be missing the last two digits sometimes, it is unknown why this happens.
Gates might sometimes not open correctly after pressing their button. This bug likely appeared due a change in Roblox's physics, and LSPLASH , the main developer, has replied to a tweet where this bug is mentioned, blaming Roblox's physics. [ 20 ]
This bug can also affect the big double door near the start of the first room.
This bug doesn't cause major problems anymore as gates become instantly non-collidable once they start moving, since the 10/19/25 patch.
When the The Halloween Spotlight Event started, a common bug appeared where Eyestalk would kill people during its ending cutscene. This was patched four days later, in the 10/27/25 patch.
The Groundskeeper can get stuck sometimes, even ignoring Mandrakes that are attacking players nearby. It can also kill players through walls.
Mandrakes might jump out at players from unreachable places or places they can't reach in time, killing them almost unavoidably, unless they somehow get away from it before dying. [ 21 ]
Interacting with a Mandrake right as the Groundskeeper is about to kill it will make the player pick up the dead Mandrake, being impossible to drop and taking away the player's ability to hide.
The Mandrake will disappear from the player's hands once the room where the Mandrake spawned despawns.
If a player steps on a Snare while a Mandrake is attacking them, that Mandrake will commonly be unable to kill the player.
Monuments rarely move without playing their footsteps sound. [ 22 ] They can also kill players through walls.
Monuments can rarely spawn incorrectly making them detach from their bases prematurely and leave a visible crack making them easier to spot. This is more commonly seen when spawning them with the Admin Panel .
Eyestalk might very rarely instantly disappear and kill everybody instantly after a few seconds. It isn't totally known why this happens, but NormallyAve , one of the developers, has spoken out about this bug and has been trying to fix it. [ 23 ]
The same bug can affect Seek in its chases too, as they work the same exact way.
Items that spawn on cabin tables have a chance of spawning too far into the cabin's corner, being unreachable.
Items that spawn in fountains have a chance of spawning too far into the fountains's center, being unreachable.
Bramble might very rarely jumpscare players even if they aren't moving, it is unknown why this happens. [ 24 ]
In games with a lot of people, cutscenes and other events might be delayed for some players by several seconds.
Monuments, Groundskeeper, Eyestalk and Bramble can lead players with God Mode into the Death Screen and damage them with their jumpscares.
If Eyestalk reaches an unopened door, it will permanently stop and will lose its ability to kill players, as it lacks the ability to open doors.
The same occurs if Eyestalk reaches the end of the chase without players triggering the cutscene.
If players use a Lotus to rebirth into The Outdoors , most signs that say the remaining distance until the exit will be broken and won't display the correct number.
For a very brief period of time, when The Great Outdoors Update released in the Lobby , players could join The Outdoors in the state it had before the update properly released (because only the Lobby had updated, not the Game place). [ 25 ]
Creating an Outdoors lobby will give a seed of -1, as well as not allowing you to advance from a certain distance
Trivia
After completing The Outdoors , players will be sent to The Mines at Jeff Shop down the sewer drain, skipping major challenging rooms such as the rest of The Greenhouse (if the player chooses to enter The Outdoors in Room 90), the final room of The Hotel featuring Figure , and the first half of The Mines, including the first Seek chase, as well as The Nest . This is however likely intended to be counterbalanced by the harsher difficulty of the subfloor, comprising of mostly instant-kill entities.
Major locations and entities in The Outdoors may have been designed to imitate the ones that would have been encountered in a normal run: The Eyestalk chase is likely meant to be the replacement for the first Seek chase encountered in The Mines , while the hedge maze has guillotines that work similarly to the anchors in The Nest , having to be searched for and then activated, albeit in a simpler manner. Bramble may have been to imitate Figure that would have been encountered earlier in The Mines.
Notably, the sewer drain the players fall through will not have a large vine holding a lamp post, which can be normally seen when progressing through The Mines normally either from The Hotel or as a new run.
This however creates a plot hole, as the players would have never encountered The Nest and the Grumbles , or even have made the Figure enter The Mines in the run, yet both appear in The Mines in pursuit of the players as usual. This is likely due to being an oversight as the developers intended to focus entirely on the subfloor. However it is possibly implied that there will potentially be new features in The Mines such as a completely different timeline when entering from the Outdoors in the future.
Unlike The Rooms and The Backdoor , where you exit via a portal from Starlight , the way of entrance and exit through The Outdoors is directly connected to The Hotel and The Mines .
Before the release of the subfloor, the big door in The Greenhouse that was going to lead to The Outdoors was only found in Room 99. After the release, both Rooms 90 and 99 contain this entrance door, most likely to allow players to enter The Outdoors without having to go through the challenging section of the Greenhouse.
Entering from either doors mostly likely do not have an impact towards the Outdoors , as both leads to the same starting room and the same amount of progression to The Mines .
The subfloor's name in the game files and in many assets is commonly mentioned as "The Garden", even before its release. Because of this, it was widely believed to be initially named "The Garden", until the final and current name was confirmed.
The date of leaks from The Outdoors have shown that the subfloor was being concepted and developed even prior to DOORS ' release .
The subfloor's first noticeable teaser was in the 6 Billion visits code announcement. [ 26 ]
The Mines was initially also teased in a similar way. [ 27 ]
Before The Mines Update , Room 99 in The Greenhouse had a door that was covered in vines, but was smaller than the current door with vines covering it, this door could have possibly been teasing the subfloor since The Hotel+ Update .
Though it was believed that this subfloor would release as part of Roblox's The Hunt: Mega Edition event, this was not the case. [ 28 ]
DOORS eventually ended up being a part of the event, however the event did not correlate with a game update; the contestants in the event played RANKED MODE . [ 29 ]
The Outdoors , despite only being a subfloor , is very unique compared to the others, and even different from the main floors in some ways.
It's the subfloor with the most entities with instakill attacks, with five entities having them.
The only hostile entities and hazards in this floor without instant killing are Surge, Snare and Bramble's spikes.
It is the first subfloor to feature major events, such as chases, cutscenes and a grand boss level.
It is the first subfloor to include over 3 unique entities and the first to include 3D entities.
It has the most achievements for a subfloor, with 5 exclusive achievements (4 if not counting "Rebirth").
The Rooms and The Backdoor have 3 and 2 exclusive achievements respectively (3 for The Backdoor if counting "THE HUNT: First Edition") .
The Outdoors is the only subfloor to feature no items directly related to Starlight, aside from Stardust .
The Backdoor has the Starlight Vial and Starlight Bottle , while The Rooms has the Starlight Jug .
The audio during the ending of the subfloor has both the sound effects and soundtrack being mixed into a single audio file, which is proven by having the music turned off in settings.
The same occurs at the start of the second Seek chase and the ending of The Mines .
If Modifiers are active in The Hotel , the entrance to The Outdoors will be blocked by a red barrier, preventing players from accessing it, similar to The Rooms .
During the initial release of The Outdoors , there was no barrier in front of the entrance, but the Skeleton Key couldn't be used on the lock.
Unlike The Rooms ' entrance, The Outdoors ' entrance is more visible in The Hotel and easier to unlock, most likely due to The Rooms being more of a hidden easter egg.
Before The Great Outdoors Update , The Ou tdoors was already in the game, but was obviously inaccessible.
During the extremely small period of time after the Lobby subplace updated and before the Game subplace updated, players could join The Outdoors . This has been documented by few, this video and this video being the only known pieces of footage of this: the version of The Outdoors that was in the game before The Great Outdoors Update was actually released.
Many rooms had the same layout as some of the rooms that ended up releasing. One room required a lever to open a gate, exactly like the basements in The Hotel. There were no major events/rooms yet. No entities (other than Hide , Glitch , Starlight as the death messenger and Snare only in the first room) were present in this version.
It is very likely that the 6B Visits Teaser was taken in this version or a very similar one, due to the colors and lightning and the shape of the tree to the left.
NormallyAve , one of the developers, has replied to a message that included one of these videos with footage of the old Outdoors , just saying "LOL". [ 30 ]
The Mill's name was teased before The Great Outdoors Update , suggesting that it was going to be a special room in The Outdoors , as it ended up being. [ 31 ]
The Outdoors is the only subfloor where the player isn't assisted by Starlight throughout the floor and the exit of the floor.
The average and approximate length of The Outdoors is 2,500m, but it can actually range from 2,050m up to 3,000m, based on currently observed runs.
The total distance will be mentioned in the first sign found at the first room, past the gates.
In total, The Outdoors has 35 rooms, 36 if counting the starting room with the fountain. you can see this in the top of the admin panel, where it shows the current room number.
During development, Lightning_Splash posted an image featuring a model of Gubby (a Roblox OC) in The Outdoors .
The Outdoors was originally supposed to have rain but the developers didn't like how it looked and therefore it was removed. Despite this, there is still rain in the first room of The Outdoors , which will slowly fade away and stop as the player steps outside. [ 32 ]
This makes it possible to leave the starting room, observe the rain stop, and then returning to the room to notice it start again.
This made Surge seem out of place. However, lightning can still occur without raining in reality, as long as the clouds are in a raining state.
In the teasers and the release date trailer for The Great Outdoors Update , the image used for The Outdoors in some of the UI would show one of the backgrounds of the Jungle biome from Terraria.
