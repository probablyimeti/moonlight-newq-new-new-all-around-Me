The Stairwell is a subfloor [ 1 ] accessed via The Mines or via the Lobby with different entities and layout that can be explored during gameplay. It is The Mines' first official subfloor.
Overview
The Stairwell is the first subfloor in The Mines . The entrance is at Door 152 and can randomly spawn in a side room after Jeff's Shop, with the entering animation looking almost exactly like the Archives entering animation but with no locks and sign. A Skeleton Key is required to access this floor. You can find an entrance by looking for a manhole cover with a Skeleton key.
It has multiple other entrances after Jeffâs Shop in The Mines. They appear as subrooms within a room and can spawn again even if you beat The Stairwell in the same run. These subrooms aren't as big as the main entrance. The manhole cover also takes much less time to walk to than the main entrance.
At the end of the Archives' Sector S is a sign pointing at vacuum, labeled "<----stairwell". The other half of this broken room can be found at the end of the Stairwell, confirming that these two subfloors were once together.
The Stairwell was a part of The Archives , as seen in a secret room at the end of The Archives and The Stairwell, but they were divided by the vacuum.
Similar to the entities in The Hotel , most of them only serve to hinder the player's progress. There are currently 6 known entities: Creak, Noise, Stems, Cobbler, Hijack, & Meld. The first attack usually happens shortly after entering the staircase or upon entering a maze floor. The first entity encountered is generally Creak, which can be spotted by its bright white eye shining in the dark.

Creak is the main antagonist and main recurring entity of The Stairwell , breaking down the large metal door after the player reaches Landing 10 and constantly stalking the player.
Appearance
Creak appears as a tall, lanky humanoid figure covered in multiple fleshy holes. Its design is based on ideas from its earlier concept stage, making it look somewhat unfinished and hollow. The holes are scattered across its body to contribute to its unsettling presence within the quiet environment of The Stairwell .
Behavior
Creak tends to remain mostly still, or moves at a slow pace. The entity only moves when a player looks away (moving closer), or looking at (backing up) him. If a player stares at it for too long, or chases it down, making it back up for too long, it will run at the player, killing them instantly upon contact. This entity also avoids light. This shy behavior fits in with the tense and quiet atmosphere of The Stairwell , mostly within the maze section where it is most commonly encountered.
Creak can open up Meld doors.
Alternatively, if Creak is hit by a crusher, it'll shove the crusher back away from it, and quickly recover from the impact.
Creak has a hidden aggression meter [ 2 ] which can't be seen by normal players, however, its arms are a visible way to tell how aggressive it is. If its hands are up to its face, it is not aggressive, but the closer its hands are to the floor, the more aggressive it is. Creak's aggression meter will slowly deplete over time when the stalked player doesn't observe it or gets too close to it.
Creak only tends to stalk one player at a time, the person being stalked should be more aware and be careful when making eye contact with it. If the stalked player has not looked at it in a while, Creak will attempt to sneak right up behind the player, it won't kill them immediately though when it does this. When sneak attacking, the stalked player can see its left hand in their field of vision, to avoid death, the player simply has to turn around and look at it. This will scare Creak off for a while and will also award the Paparazzi badge.
Notes
If a player takes too long to leave a landing after a fire alarm is pulled, they may have to deal with Creak once again while advancing to the next landing.
Getting behind Creak (such as going down the stairs) will result in an instant death, no matter if its aggression meter is high or not, but if you are playing with someone else and they are being stalked by Creak , getting behind Creak will only result in an instant death when it approaches you.
Creak is able to force closed Meld open in an attempt to reach the player. Once Creak makes its way through the Meld, the latter will close shortly after.
Creak will also open these Melds when it is backing away.
Do not stare at Creak for a prolonged period of time, as this will increase its aggressiveness rapidly.
Being too close to Creak will also increase its aggressiveness.
When Creak opens the blast doors, he will instantly kill you if you are watching him spawn.
If you don't force open the checkpoint door for long enough, Creak will teleport to you and instantly kill you.

Strategy
Walking towards Creak will make it slowly back off towards the landing's entrance door. When at low aggression, looking at it for a few seconds will scare it off, causing it to run several flights of stairs back. This will also happen if a player looks at Creak while it is quietly sneaking up on them. However, looking at it for too long will cause its aggression levels to rise. The lower its arms are, the higher its aggression is. Its aggression levels will slowly go down when not stared at.
If its aggression levels are too high while the player is looking at it, it will rush the player and kill them. This can also happen when not looking at Creak for too long, except it will not kill the player instantly when it reaches them, instead it will be heard sprinting at a fast speed up the stairs towards the player. Once it reaches the player, it will try to sneak attack them from behind. Its left hand will come into the player's view, and if the player doesn't turn around, Creak will kill them. If the player does turn around, Creak will be scared off.
If you are playing solo and feel scared of Creak , try to think of them as a "friend". Even though Creak is the main antagonist of the Stairwell, just try to imagine that Creak is giving you company as you traverse the Stairwell as long as you don't provoke it.
Trivia
Creak' s model has always been in the game since August 10, 2022 (the beginning of Doors's Development) originally known as Abraham (named after Abraham Lincoln, the 16th president of the U.S.) and was originally a prototype of The Figure , before being revamped in The Archive Update from Abraham to Creak .
It is heavily likely that Creak is somehow related to or influenced by Meld.
Creak is made of stone, as stated in the Journal .
Creak' s mechanic is similar to the Bracken from Lethal Company.
Unlike the Bracken, however, Creak won't become hostile when looked at for too long and will only attack when backed into a corner under a specific circumstance.
Creak' s face was teased on a painting in The Outdoors .
Creak' s feet were seen at the end of The Archives trailer as well as its silhouette able to be seen in the beginning of the Archives teaser.

Meld is the overarching antagonist of The Stairwell . It is usually neutral and can appear in the Depot Rooms, slowing your progression until destroyed. It is implied to be the reason Creak is hostile to players.
Appearance
Meld is a meat fungus which has "infected" the Stairwell. It takes the appearance of meat similar to that of Figure's, however more liquid-ish and having more biological features. Meld can be found in the form of a door throughout some landings.
Behavior
Meld can be opened by interacting with white string-like structures that are nearby. The player is not allowed to go to the next landing until Meld is gone. Once incinerated by the Fire Alarm, players can use the Emergency Override feature on the gate Terminal.
Alternatively, the player can use an item that emits fire to force it to open, such as a lit Candle , Flare . Lighter , or Lantern , but that may enrage the entity, and will make it use it's veins to wrap around the player who offended them and slow them down.
Notes
Creak is able to open and close the Meld doors at will when chasing the player or backing away.
Meld can be forcefully opened with the help of a lit Candle , Lighter , or Lantern .
Meld now grows, dripping from the ceiling and reaching out to you after the ACCESS GRANTED update, which may imply it's actually alive.

Only force open Meld in an attempt to escape when being chased by entities like Hijack , Creak or when overwhelmed by Stem(s) .

Noise is the secondary antagonist and an common recurring entity of The Stairwell , guaranteed to always spawn inside of a Depot Room and appearing whenever players approaches the television's line of sight.
Appearance
Noise is a non-distinct humanoid made of static.
Behavior
When approaching one of the many televisions throughout the landings of The Stairwell , Noise will emerge from it and start following all players. Every player has their own instance of Noise , which spectating players can see as well. Only one instance of Noise can appear at a time for each player.
Noise will move on the same path that the player takes, at a constant speed. Standing still will cause Noise to pause in place, only resuming once the player starts moving again. When Noise gets too far from its player, it will temporarily speed up to catch up to them, ignoring if the player is moving or not.
Noise can be destroyed by two means: Either by rolling the television it spawned from down a set of stairs, or by letting it get burned by standing outside a safe room after the fire alarm has been triggered.
Colliding with Noise at any time results in an instant kill, which will also remove all other players' Noise .
Notes
If you insert the disc found in The Archives into Noise's television, Noise will reverse back into it and re-emerge as Hijack .

When you are too far away from Noise and it starts to speed up, immediately start looping, forcing Noise to try and catch up with you.
Sometimes, Noise may end up yellow, this may be caused if the player uses a Flare near Noise.

Hijack is a fusion between Noise and Ransom , created when bringing the Disc from The Archives and inserting it in Noise's television.
Appearance
Hijack appears as a red-like static humanoid entity with Ransom 's facial expression flashing multiple times in the entity's face.
Sometimes, when Hijack stops in place for a few seconds, a red octagonal stop sign suddenly appears inside the entity's torso.
Behavior
To activate this entity, you have to make your way to Room S-050 in The Archives , where you can find a laptop with Ransom inside. Interacting with the laptop, you will then have to click on the "S" logo on the bottom left side of the screen and press "Eject". Doing so will reveal a disk labelled " CD-1 ".
After grabbing the said disk, you'll have to make your way to The Stairwell and find Noise's television to inject the disk into it. Noise will immediately retract back to its television and the television will display a video before it gets hijacked by Ransom and summoning Hijack .
Unlike Noise , Hijack is much faster and won't stop moving when the player stands still, instead, will randomly stop moving for short intervals, lasting a random amount of time. After each interval, it may change its running speed.
Upon successfully destroying the initial Hijack , Ransom has a chance to attack during every Noise encounter. If it catches the player moving, instead of forcing them to pay a ransom, it will infect Noise and will turn it into Hijack once again.
Notes
Similarily to Noise , all active Hijacks will immediately despawn if any player dies to one of them.
If multiple Noises are present (Happens when you skip a landing without destroying Noise after destroying the first Hijack ) , if Ransom catches the player moving, all active Noises will turn into Hijack .
Strategy
It is always reccomended to bring Items that inflicts speed boosts on yourself when Hijack is active.
If you are going to activate Hijack , make sure to have one of your allies leading Noise far from its television. This allows you and your allies to quickly search for the fire alarm and beat Hijack quickly.

Stem is one of the common recurring entities in The Stairwell , having a chance to appear when opening a container.
Appearance
Stem (s) appears as clay-like entities with bumps on them and are shaped as a sphere with the following colors: Orange, Green, Blue, and rarely Gold, some of them having parts of the human body, like eyes, noses, ears, mouths, or none at all.
Behavior
When opening one of the containers (Also known as Cubbies for the Stems) around The Depot room, 1-4 Stems will have a chance to spawn inside of that opened container. They will roll to make their way towards the nearest player, ramming them with their body or attempting to bite them (If they have mouths) , dealing 5 Health per hit.
You can destroy Stem (s) by interacting with them and dropping/throwing them four times.
If enough stems cluster up, they can group into different Stem Structures , each stem serving as a body part. These include Rotators , Eye Towers , Stem People , and Stem Faces . These names are unofficial.
Stem Structures are docile and won't harm the player at all, unless you bother them by grabbing one of them.

Cobbler is an entity in The Stairwell . It can be seen as an sillhouette throughout The Stairwells and can be found in Landing 99.
Appearance
Cobbler appears to be a dark translucent, hooded entity with a cryptic symbol on its face, which translates to the letter 'I' in the entity's alphabet (Cobblish). The entity also has bandages wrapped around it's limbs, a flute on its left hip and wearing socks.
Behavior
Cobbler is a passive entity, sitting at the end of The Stairwells (Landing 99) , it will point directly to the fire alarm once the player reaches at Landing 99 until the player pulls it, which allows you to interact with the entity. Upon interacting with it, it starts to speak with you in it's own language, but if you have any scrap in your inventory, you can trade 1 with Cobbler for 10.
If you head over the shelf (Aka Cobbler's Shop) , and purchase the Scanner , Cobbler will instantly teleport once you purchase the item and will give you 2 modules to insert into it, here is a list of them below:
Flashlight Module: Acts as a Flashlight whenever it is toggled
Threat Detector Module: Will detect any entity, including friendly ones, through the Scanner's FOV when inserted
Translator Module: Translates the speech of the Cobbler when interacting with them
Notes
In the room/landing where Cobbler spawns (last landing before exit to the mines) there is a shelf. The shelf stores the Starlight Barrel ( 750), Peach Cobbler ( 250), and the Radar ( 500).
This landing was also connected to The Archives , but it has been broken by the Vacuum .

The Fire Sprinkler is the major Hazard in The Stairwell , they can be seen on the roofs of Depot Rooms.
Appearance
The Fire Sprinklers appears as silver, cylindrical shaped sprinklers built into the roof of The Stairwell . They are basically everywhere in the Depot Room and will burn down both the Staircase Modules and the Depot Rooms with fire.
Behavior
These Fire Sprinklers are activated when triggering the Fire Alarm inside of one Fire Station around the Depot Room. Once the alarm is triggered, a siren will begin blaring, giving a warning to all players that the Fire Sprinklers will incinerate the entirety of the Depot Room. After 12 seconds, the blast doors of the Fire Escapes will close down to protect everything inside of them, and the Fire Sprinklers will be activated, inflicting a long burning effect to players outside the Fire Escapes, temporarily stunning Creak, destroying all televisions, and incinerating Meld.
Large Items (Also known as "Depot Items") that are outside of the Fire Escapes will visibly burn and turn into piles of ash.

You are able to survive the Fire Sprinkler with the help of 1 Starlight Bottle and a full pack of bandages .

Garage Doors can spawn in maze landings. They can be opened using a garage key, which has a chance to spawn in the various cubbies found around the landing. Room Mechanics
Fire Alarm
Interacting with a Fire Alarm will cause the entire landing to set off alarms. All "Fire Escape" rooms will close within fifteen seconds. After blast doors are secured, the landing will incinerate any Meld within the level. After this, you can use the Emergency Override feature on the terminal next to the gate. To survive incineration, players must locate and stay in a Fire Escape room until the process is complete. It is guaranteed for a Fire Alarm to spawn in a room containing meld.
Depot
You can pick up trash lying around the floor and drop them off at a Depot for Scraps. You will get the Paperclip Challenge badge for using a Depot for the first time. Each disposable item dropped off rewards 1-5 scrap. If you scrap a Ceramic Stem , it will reward you 1 scrap for every stem. If you incinerate an Eye Tower , Stem Person , or a Stem Face , they will give you specific amounts of scrap, respectively.

Tips
If you rely heavily on lighting sources, try bringing a Battery Pack with some spare batteries to recharge them.
It is strongly recommended to bring a long-range light source; the Flashlight , Bulklight , Straplight , and Shakelight are the most suitable options.
The Shakelight is especially useful, since it does not need batteries.
You can use a shopping cart to transport the scrap you've found to the depot more efficiently. However, the lack of movement while carrying it can leave you exposed to Noise andâif you don't keep a close watch on your surroundingsâto Creak .
You can also store items inside of the shopping cart, leaving you more space on your hotbar.
You can also carry scraps using the briefcase, allowing you transport scraps to a shopping cart more efficiently.
If you're having trouble with Stem , carry a Bandage Pack with a large supply of bandages, although it will be useless against Creak , and Noise , since they can kill you instantly.
Some Meld movable walls react to the cords; for example, interacting with a cord might cause one wall to open while another closes, or vice versa. Therefore, be patient while waiting for a wall to open, or look for alternative routes. Remember; Noise and Creak can go through Meld walls whenever they like.
You can force open Meld using Flare , Lighter or Lantern , as they are weak to fire.

Trivia
The Stairwell used to have ~200 landings, but this was changed to ~100 on the 5th of October, 2026. This was likely to reduce the amount of time spent walking between terminals.
The amount of rooms in between each terminal was decreased because of this.
The way you activate Hijack is similar to Spamton NEO from Deltarune ,taking one character ,putting them onto a disc, and putting them into a device,altering them.
Stem Constructions have collisions with things able to be pushed. This includes Chairs, Trolleys, ect.
It also includes Noise's TV Stand, allowing for a very unique way of destroying it.
This can be achieved by pushing it over a Rotator Stem Construction.
