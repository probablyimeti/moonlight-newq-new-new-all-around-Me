"Listen closely for Rush and stay near hiding spots." - Guiding Light
Hiding is a basic mechanic which allows players to utilize specific pieces of furniture to survive from certain entities .
Overview
When the player is near a hiding spot like a wardrobe or a bed, the hiding ( ) icon will appear. By pressing E on PC, the " hiding button " on Mobile or on Xbox, it will play a short animation of the player entering the closet, or crawling underneath the bed (for The Hotel, or The Backdoor [only closet]), locker or fridge (The Rooms), or entering the lockers, crawling into sewage grates, or getting into the dumpsters. To exit a hiding spot, players will need to walk forward or press the exit button on mobile.

Notes
The only spots considered hiding spots by the game are wardrobes, beds and lockers.
However, it is possible to hide from Rush , Ambush and similar entities without hiding by staying out of their line of sight. Doing this for the first time awards the player with the In Plain Sight achievement .
Once the player is inside a closet or underneath a bed, the game will consider the player hidden.
As of The Hotel+ Update , hiding after Screech appears will despawn it.
Out of every hiding spot, Jack can only appear inside wardrobes (The Hotel) and lockers (The Mines).
The player was unable to hide in a closet during Seek 's chase, this is due to a bug forcing the player to constantly move forwards, therefore the player will always exit any closets.
As of The Hotel+ Update and prior to The Content Update , the player needs to hold W to run, so the player is able to hide in a closet during a Seek Chase, if there are any at all.
Seek is still able to kill hiding players, instantly triggering its jumpscare upon interaction with the hiding spot.
This may also be a reference to the phrase "You can run, but you can't hide (although the "hide" is not present)" mentioned in Seek's achievement, since the player can run to survive Seek but they can't hide from it.
Only one player can hide in each hiding spot. Multiple players cannot hide together.
This is not the case in 2024 April Fools Event .
Double pillow bed lets 3 players in at a time.
The dumpster in The Mines lets 2 players in at a time.
Wardrobes and Lockers can not be entered from behind. Doing so will grant the player with a block symbol in their screen that flashes slowly in red and white. However, they can be entered from the sides.
This is not the case when playing in Hotel- Modifier.
In one of the rooms within The Rooms subfloor, there is a locker colored white to mimic the style of a fridge.
There are also a type of unusable locker within a room type called "Broken Lockers" which the player cannot hide in.
This type of locker is more known as a Blue locker.
Players may hide in lockers for as long as they wish in The Rooms, The Backdoor , and The Outdoors , as Hide is not present on these floors .
However, you can't hide for too long in The Backdoor , due to the presence of Haste .
The player used to be able to hide under the beds in The Infirmary before The Hunt Update , but this feature was removed for unknown reasons.
Hiding time will increase if Ambush spawns. If Ambush despawns after, the hiding time will be set to default.
Jeff The Killer can still stab you through a closet even if you are hiding.

Bugs
There used to be a bug where hiding under a bed would result in the player 's camera clipping through the floor. This has since been fixed.
There is a bug, when leaving under the bed, the camera will not have animation.
There is a bug where the player character's camera will move similar to walking animation, before exiting the closet.
If a player hides while holding a Puzzle Painting , the player's point of view will tilt awkwardly.
There is a bug where if the player hides under a bed behind a big sized window but on top of the bed, the animation has a chance to glitch out and make the player stuck outside.
There is a bug with beds, where if the player hides near the corner, exits, and during the exit animation does a "freeze tab glitch", it's possible to go through the floor, causing the player's death or Glitch to appear and teleport them. This bug is not consistent and it is hard to replicate.
Being in a hiding animation right when Void's teleport occurs will make the mentioned teleport fail, causing the player to fall into the void (being teleported by Glitch right after).
Gold used to have a big hitbox that could block raycasting from node entities, being able to survive more easily without hiding .
This was fixed in The Mines Update . It still works in Hotel- and in Super Hard Mode .
Drawers in The Backdoor and in The Mines could block raycasting from node entities, being able to survive more easily without hiding .
Both of these have been fixed in The Content Update .
Snares used to have a hitbox that blocked raycasting big enough to make players able to survive just standing of them, although needing to step on them first once to stand in the middle safely and carefuly after, making survival more easy without needing to hide .
This was fixed in The Mines Update . It still works in Super Hard Mode .
Naturally spawned items (not dropped by players) used to have a big hitbox that could block raycasting from node entities, being able to survive more easily without hiding .
This bug only started working in The Mines Update , but was fixed in The Content Update .
In PARTY MODE , raycasting from node entities can be blocked just by holding a Hiding Box or a Throwable Prop, being able to survive more easily without hiding .
The Hiding Box is able to block ray-casting from both the front and the back of the player, but not their sides.
Throwable Props are able to block ray-casting only from the front of the player, being vulnerable from every other direction.
Being ragdolled might sometimes break the item's hitbox causing it to not block the raycasting correctly.
Pressing the interaction button in a hiding spot multiple times while Screech is following the player will make the message "Something is stopping you from hiding..." appear, despite being able to hide .
When hiding while in FLY/NOCLIP mode with the Admin Panel , your camera will move by itself rapidly.
Trivia
It is possible to complete a round of DOORS without interacting with any hiding spots by being out of range or sight when Rush or Ambush attacks and keeping a distance when Figure appears.
There is a
1
200
{\displaystyle \frac{1}{200}}
chance that Jack will appear to block players from hiding in a wardrobe ( Hotel ) or a locker ( Mines ).
Jack can't spawn if the latest open room is one that Rush or Ambush spawned in.
If you open the next door before you hide, you have a chance of finding Jack , which is why you should try not to open the next door after Rush or Ambush spawns.
The animation of hiding in a closet is similar to the animation of hiding in a closet and locker from CASE: Animatronics.
Players were able to crawl into vents to hide in plain sight before Vent Rooms ' removal.
Hide is not present in closets while Figure is active in The Electrical Room , so players may hide for as long as they wish.
In SUPER HARD MODE , due to the exit door not closing if a Holy Hand Grenade is used on Figure in the library, Hide will not appear for the rest of the game.
Redibles has claimed to have ideas for uses for hiding under beds. [ 1 ]
After The Hotel+ Update , Rush and Ambush can now spawn in rooms that have beds, giving beds a use. However, if Ambush were to appear, it's recommended to hide in a closet as the bed animation takes longer than the closet animation.
It should be noted however, that the player is immune to the entities during the animations. However, the player is not immune to Rush nor Ambush during the exiting animation of the closet, despite being immune to other entities, such as Eyes .
There was initially going to be a Jack-like mechanic for beds, although it was scrapped due to LSPLASH not following through, although it may come back at a later time. [ 2 ]
Before The Mines Update , lockers from The Rooms used to not have an animation, this is possibly to mimic the way that a player hides in lockers in the original Rooms created by nicorocks5555.
In Rooms by nicorocks5555, however, to hide, the player need to sit on a 'Seat' part that is inside lockers, which will give players invulnerability to entities when sat on.
The hiding in closet animation opens the door to the next room if the closet is close to it.
The same goes with lockers in The Rooms with teleportation of player's character in the locker.
With The Hunt Update , camera rotation inside hiding spots has increased.
In PARTY MODE , every hiding sound is sped up.
